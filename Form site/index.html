Before you send

The questions owners usually ask. Tap one to open it.

Do I need internet all the time?

No. The till keeps taking sales when the signal drops — they are held on the device and upload by themselves once you are back online. Nothing is lost and you do not have to do anything.

You do need a signal the first time you open it on a device, and it is worth opening it once a day with internet so everything catches up.

What device does it run on?

Any phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser. Chrome is the safest choice. You open a web address once, then save it to the home screen and it behaves like a normal app.

A cheap Android tablet propped on the counter is what most shops use. The device is yours to buy — it is not part of the system price.

Do I need a receipt printer?

No. Plenty of shops run without one and just tell the customer the total. If you want printed receipts, a small thermal printer with a 58 mm roll is the usual choice, and you can add one later without changing anything in the till.

Can I add products or change prices myself later?

Yes, and you will. Everything you type here is only the starting list. With your admin code you can add a product, change a price, put something on sale or mark an item out of stock, any day, from the till itself. You are never waiting on Reymar for a price change.

What if my menu isn’t ready yet?

Send the form without it. You will get a short sample list to replace at your own pace. Typing it here is faster, though — the prices go in once and the till is usable the moment you open it.

Who else can see my sales?

Only you and whoever you give the sign-in to. Your shop gets its own database, opened under your own email, holding only your shop’s records.

Reymar looks at it when you ask for help and only for as long as that takes. Your sales, products, staff and customer records are yours, and you can export all of it to a spreadsheet whenever you like.

Can two cashiers use it at the same time?

Yes. Sign in on as many devices as you need and they all see the same products, stock and sales. Queue numbers are handed out by the server, so two counters can never give the same number to different customers.

Can it keep track of utang?

Yes. You can save a customer, ring up a sale as credit, and the amount sits on their record until they pay. You see who owes what and how long it has been outstanding without keeping a notebook.

Can I use this for official BIR receipts?

No. Arroyo POS prints an order receipt for your customer and keeps your own sales records. It is not a BIR-accredited system, so it does not stand in for official receipts or invoices you are required to issue.

If your shop is registered for those, carry on issuing them the way you do now. The till’s reports make your own bookkeeping easier either way.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not for the software. You pay once for it and nothing more is owed for using it — no monthly charge, no yearly charge, no cut of your sales.

What you carry yourself is the ordinary running cost: your internet, and your own hardware.

What happens when the free service period ends?

The till keeps working exactly as before and all your data stays with you. What stops is service — fixing faults, updates and support — until you arrange to continue it.

You will be told the price in writing before the free period runs out, and continuing is your choice, not automatic.

I answered something wrong. Can I change it?

Yes. Nothing is locked in until your till is built. Fill the form in again and send it, or just message Reymar with the correction.

Is anything charged through this form?

No. The form asks for no card details and takes no payment. It only collects what your till needs to be set up.
